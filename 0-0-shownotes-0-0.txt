Opening / Random Block & Kitty's Video
Before we get started... I need to make a quick announcement.
Right before I walked out here, I opened up the player list, picked one person in this room completely at random, and I blocked you.
So for the next ten minutes, this entire set is going to be a complete, silent mystery to them because they are looking at an empty stage while everyone around them is laughing. So if you see someone next to you looking around completely lost going, "Wait, what is going on? Why is everyone laughing?"... do not ruin this for me, okay? Let them wander in the dark. It is funny to me, and that's what matters.
Now, now I started thinking about this set  tonight  because Kitty sent me a video earlier one of those classic "Customer States" videos online. And it immediately gave me PTSD from back when I used to work in an auto shop. So I figured I'd reminisce a bit and share some of the absolute wild moments from those garage days with you guys. 

I won’t name the exact shop I worked at for legal reasons, but let’s just say… we were pretty big on tires.
And when you work in a shop, customer service requires you to keep a straight face while dealing with people who should not be trusted with sharp objects, let alone vehicles.
We had a lady come in one day, absolutely furious. She had paid a ton of money the day before to get her AC fixed. She storms back into the shop, screaming that we scammed her, broke her car, and that her air conditioning wasn't working at all.
Now, it is 95 degrees outside. Sweat is dripping off my forehead onto the pavement. I walk out to her car, brace myself for a massive repair disaster, lean into the driver's side window... and I look at the climate control dial.
She has it cranked all the way to surface of the sun.
Car climate controls are not complicated! You get two choices: Blue and Red. Blue is "Arctic Tundra," Red is "Blast Furnace." There is no secret third option! But she had it set so far into the red that the cabin was literally hot enough to bake a batch of Toll House cookies on the dashboard.
And I’m standing there in the heat, trying to hold down my professional retail face while every fiber of my being wants to scream, "Lady... you have it set to plasma!"
I had to physically reach in, turn the dial three inches to the left, feel the ice-cold air blast out, and look at her like, "There you go, ma'am. That'll be zero dollars."
And as she drove off, it hit me: This person gets to vote. And she operates a two-ton metal box on the highway.

That wasn't even the wildest thing that happened at that shop.
One day, a guy brings in a 40-passenger van. Now, if you’ve never seen a 40-passenger van, this thing isn't a vehicle it’s a small aircraft carrier. It’s so long that the front bumper is watching the sunrise while the back bumper is watching the moon set. It takes three business days just to walk around it.
He brings it in for brake work. We finish the job, and protocol says you have to take it on a test drive to make sure the brakes actually, you know, stop the land-yacht.
Well, the tech taking it out gives it a little too much... gumption. A little too much of the beans. It had been raining, the road was slick, and he manages to slides the van completely off the road into a ditch.
Now, a normal person in this scenario stops, takes a breath, and calls the shop: "Hey, bring your truck jon and some chains, I'm stuck."
Not this guy. This guy was a firm believer in the Jeremy Clarkson philosophy of problem-solving: MORE POWER.
If spinning the wheels at 2,000 RPM didn't work, surely redlining it at 6,000 RPM will magically turn liquid soup into dry pavement! He floorboards it. White smoke, mud flying 30 feet in the air, engine screaming. By the time he gives up, the van isn't just stuck—it’s sitting frame-deep in the earth. The entire side of this white  van looks like a Jackson Pollock painting.
We had to drag it out, haul it back to the bay, and  power-wash a small mountain of topsoil off the side. There was literally a river of mud flowing down the parking lot to the drain.
The customer comes back to pick it up, and I handed him the keys with a straight face and said: "Yeah, man, it looked a little dusty out there, so we decided to give you a free courtesy wash."
He thanked us and drove away! Never noticed the river of mud and chunks of dirt in the parking lot 

Then there was the guy who pulled in one afternoon with four flat tires.
Now, four flats at once is an achievement. That takes dedication. That’s not a nail in the road; that’s a lifestyle choice.
I walk out to look at the car, and I look at the front tire... and the sidewall is just gone. Sidewall.exe has stopped responding. It has exited the chat. Somewhere back on the interstate, there is a loose ring of rubber doing 45 miles an hour next to a family in a minivan.
It’s just a wheel, a strip of tread, and open air. 
I look up at the guy, and with a completely straight face, he goes: "Can you just put some air in them?"
I said, "Sir... you are missing a complete quadrant of your tire. If I attach an air hose to this, I am literally just inflating the state."
He got furious and drove away on four  rims!
And the phone calls we got at the desk were no better. I had a guy call asking for directions to the shop. So I give him our exact street address, give him the main cross street.
And without missing a single beat, he goes: "Oh, so you're right down the street from the fuck-hotel, right?"
How do you stay professional on a recorded customer line when someone uses a local hourly motel's reputation as a navigational landmark?
I am sitting there at the counter, gripping the phone, trying so hard not to lose my job, going: "Uh... yes, sir. We are located approximately three blocks north of... that establishment."
As soon as I hung up that phone, I immediately clocked out and took my lunch break. I was like, "I am NOT going to be the guy standing at the front desk when the 'Fuck-Hotel' guy pulls in."

Now for the weirdest moment 
So one afternoon, we have a packed house. Mothers, toddlers, families just waiting for their oil changes. Over in the corner, there is an older gentleman he is enough to be my grandfather staring intently at his phone on the shop Wi-Fi.
After a few minutes, he gets up, walks right past a mother with a stroller, comes straight up to the counter, and goes, "Hey, kid... I got a question for you."
Now, I'm thinking he's about to ask how much longer his car is going to take, or maybe, "Hey, I noticed this noise, can you check my brakes?" Normal shop stuff.
No. He proceeds to go, "Have you ever heard about Chaturbate?"... as he turns his phone around.
And right there on the screen is a split-haired, dyed goth woman furiously going to town on herself with silicone equipment. It was aggressive. It was highly spirited. It was an athletic event.
And this sweet, grandfatherly man is just showing me this screen in a lobby full of families!
I am trapped behind the counter. There is a toddler three feet away eating Cheerios. I just stood there, nodded slowly, and went, "Interesting. Sir, your alignment should be done in about twenty minutes."
And then I turned around and walked straight out into the garage. Nope. Couldn't do it. Was NOT staying behind that counter for another second!
(Grins, leans into the mic)
Though, hey... if you happen to be that split-haired goth girl from that stream and you somehow know what I'm talking about... hit me up. You seem like my type.
Anyway, thank you guys so much for listening to my crazy life stories! Surprisingly, none of those even involved any of my insane exes. Hopefully you guys found these just as funny as I did. I’m Big Jon, enjoy the rest of the show!

What Makes Kitty Growl

**WHAT MAKES KITTY GROWL**

You know what makes Kitty growl? People who are completely fucking unprepared for an event they have personally chosen to participate in. That is apparently my thing. And nowhere is this more obvious than the fucking drive-thru.

You pulled into the drive-thru. Nobody kidnapped you. Nobody put a gun to your head and said, “GET IN THAT FUCKING HONDA, WE’RE GOING TO BURGER KING.” You chose this. You got in line. You watched the cars ahead of you order. You slowly crept toward the giant illuminated menu displaying all of the food sold at the restaurant you deliberately drove to. And then you get to the speaker and suddenly it’s, “Uhhhhhhhhhh... can I have a minute?” A MINUTE?! What have you been doing?! You’ve been sitting in line at McDonald’s for six fucking minutes! Did you think there was going to be a quiz at the end?!

And listen, it is 2026. You have a phone. I know you have a phone. It’s probably in your hand right fucking now. That phone has the internet. The internet has A&W. A&W has a menu. Wendy’s has a menu. Popeyes has a menu. KFC has a menu. Taco Bell has a menu. Most of these places have a whole fucking app where you can lovingly assemble your little meal in complete privacy without involving another human being.

You can sit in the parking lot at Harvey’s and debate the philosophical differences between onion rings and fries for as long as your heart desires. Take twenty minutes. Make a spreadsheet. Consult your family. Call a fucking meeting. Compare sauces. Debate whether you really need the extra cheese. But when you pull up to the speaker at Wendy’s? ORDER THE FOOD.

And most of you KNOW what’s there! These menus have barely fucking changed! Nobody pulls into A&W and suddenly discovers Teen Burgers. “Holy shit, honey! They have Teen Burgers here!” YES. THEY HAVE HAD THE FUCKING TEEN BURGER. You knew that when you left your house. Nobody gets to Taco Bell and acts shocked that there are tacos. Nobody gets to Popeyes and goes, “Oh wow, chicken!” Nobody gets to Wendy’s and is stunned to discover fucking hamburgers. What the fuck did you think was going to happen?

Meanwhile there are people inside standing at a POS waiting for you to complete what should have been a two-minute interaction while seventeen other things need doing because fast-food restaurants are staffed like somebody lost a bet. There are dishes. There’s food to make. There’s shit to clean. There are delivery orders going off. There are three different timers screaming. Somebody needs something stocked. Somebody else is on break. And Brenda is at the KFC speaker going, “Do you guys have fries?” NO BRENDA, ACTUALLY WE DISCONTINUED POTATOES.

And you know what’s somehow even worse? THE PAYMENT WINDOW. Because we already told you the total. At the speaker. You heard it. You said, “Okay.” Then you drove your vehicle twenty feet. You have now arrived at the portion of the Wendy’s experience where money is traditionally exchanged for goods and services. Why, then, when I open the window, do you look FUCKING SHOCKED?

“Oh! Just a second!” A SECOND FOR WHAT?! WHY IS YOUR DEBIT CARD IN THE TRUNK?! You knew this moment was coming! You had one job during the seven seconds it took you to drive from the Taco Bell speaker to the window. Locate payment. That’s it. I’m not asking you to perform surgery. Tap the card. Take the Crunchwrap. Fuck off.

But no. Now we’re digging through the purse. Moving the coffee. Checking the cup holder. Opening the wallet. Going through twelve cards. “Oh, I think I have exact change.” PLEASE DON’T. PLEASE GOD. There are nine cars behind you at Popeyes and your chicken sandwich is aging in real time.

And honestly, at this point, why aren’t all of you ordering on the fucking app? Pick the food. Customize the food. Pay for the food. Show up. Receive food. LEAVE. Technology has eliminated nearly every obstacle between you and a cheeseburger, a bucket of chicken, a Whopper, a Teen Burger, a Wendy’s combo, a Crunchwrap Supreme or whatever the fuck else you’re after, and somehow some of you have turned purchasing lunch into an escape room.

And while we’re talking fast food, people who order burgers during breakfast. Okay. This one might be petty. I’ll admit that. Because technically, if the restaurant says you can order a burger at breakfast, then you can order a burger at breakfast. I understand this intellectually. Emotionally? Fuck your burger.

Because breakfast is set up. Eggs? Beautiful. Bacon? Ready. Hash browns? Let’s fucking go. And then somebody rolls into A&W at 7:13 in the morning like, “Can I get a Teen Burger, no Teen sauce, no onions, extra pickles, add mustard, no bacon...” WHY ARE YOU LIKE THIS?

Now we’re dragging out shit that wasn’t being used. Opening things. Setting things up. Using condiments that have limited hold times so we can make one lonely, weirdly customized fucking hamburger for Steve, who woke up today and apparently decided eggs are beneath him. Again, YOU ARE ALLOWED TO DO THIS. That’s what makes it worse. I can’t even be right about being angry. I just have to stand there thinking, “I hope you enjoy your perfectly legitimate, unnecessarily complicated breakfast hamburger, you fucking asshole.”

But it’s not even just drive-thrus. It’s people who don’t listen. You can give somebody an instruction. They will look directly at you. They will acknowledge the instruction. And then immediately do something completely different.

I have worked drive-thru and discovered you could probably say basically ANYTHING through that headset because people aren’t actually listening. “Welcome to Burger King, how can we McDonald’s you today?” They just start ordering. Nothing. Not even a twitch. I could say, “Welcome to Wendy’s, the building is currently on fire and there’s a raccoon holding the manager hostage,” and somebody would just go, “Yeah, can I get a number three?” Nobody fucking listens.

You know what else makes Kitty growl? People who make everything MORE FUCKING CHAOTIC than it needs to be. Especially when something gets a little busy at work and somebody immediately starts yelling. WHY ARE WE YELLING?! Nothing is on fire! We got BUSY. That’s it! There are customers! Apparently customers occasionally visit businesses! Who could have fucking predicted this?!

And yelling NEVER FUCKING HELPS. Nobody has ever been standing there thinking, “Shit, I’m really struggling to keep up with these orders. You know what would improve my performance? Somebody screaming behind me.” Oh, thank GOD you started shouting! I couldn’t find the fucking pickles until you raised your voice! Suddenly I can move twice as fast because you’re having a nervous breakdown beside the fryer! Calm the fuck down. Do the next thing. Then do the thing after that. We’re making hamburgers, Brenda. We’re not landing a fucking 747 with one engine.

And while we’re on the subject of completely unnecessary chaos, can we talk about the person who waits until you post something in Discord and then immediately posts SEVENTEEN FUCKING THINGS underneath it? I spent time on that post. I formatted it. I made it pretty. I put the information in there. I hit send. I sit back and think, “There. People will actually see that.” And then here comes fucking Captain Content. MESSAGE. MESSAGE. SONG. MEME. YOUTUBE VIDEO. GIF. ANOTHER SONG. RANDOM THOUGHT. PICTURE OF A CAT. SOME SHIT THAT COULD HAVE BEEN ONE MESSAGE BUT FOR SOME REASON NEEDED TO BE FUCKING NINE.

And my post is now in fucking ARCHAEOLOGY. Nobody saw it. Nobody reacted to it. It’s twelve miles up the channel being studied by historians. And THEN somebody asks a question that was ANSWERED IN THE FUCKING POST. “When does it start?” IT’S IN THE POST. “Where is it?” IT’S IN THE POST. “Who’s hosting?” IT. IS. IN. THE. FUCKING. POST. You know what? I don’t know anymore. Maybe we’re meeting behind a Wendy’s at midnight. Figure it the fuck out.

And speaking of people who apparently cannot absorb information, can we talk about asking a question that has JUST BEEN ANSWERED? Not yesterday. Not last week. I mean the information is DIRECTLY ABOVE YOUR MESSAGE. You could move your eyeballs four inches north. That’s it. No research required. No Google. No app. No degree. Just SCROLL THE FUCK UP.

And this is really the thing, isn’t it? Apparently what makes Kitty growl is having to spend extra fucking time on things because somebody else couldn’t be bothered to use five seconds of theirs. You didn’t look at the menu, so now everybody waits. You didn’t get your card ready, so now everybody waits. You didn’t read the post, so now somebody has to explain it again. You didn’t listen the first time, so now somebody has to repeat themselves. You got mildly overwhelmed, so now you’re screaming and everybody around you gets to be overwhelmed too.

And THEN there are people who don’t understand the concept of TEAMWORK. Now, I’m not even talking about Fortnite specifically because this shit exists EVERYWHERE. Games. Work. Communities. Projects. Whatever. There will be a plan. Everybody agrees to the plan. “Okay, this is what we’re doing.” Great. And then ONE MOTHERFUCKER apparently hears, “Everybody go do whatever the fuck you want and we’ll meet back here after the consequences.”

Then something goes wrong and suddenly it’s, “GUYS?!” NO. NO “GUYS.” You LEFT THE GUYS. THE GUYS WERE OVER HERE. You cannot voluntarily fuck off into the wilderness and then be surprised to discover that the team you abandoned is not somehow magically inside your asshole waiting to rescue you.

And while we’re talking technology for just one second, I have to say this because somebody somewhere is going to be mad about it. iPhones. I don’t fucking get it. I had one. I did my time. I served my sentence. And yes, they’re perfectly functional phones. Congratulations. Your phone phones. But people act like owning an iPhone is a fucking personality trait.

“Oh, you have an Android?” Yes, bitch. I enjoy being able to do whatever the fuck I want with the expensive computer I purchased. “But the bubbles are green.” I DON’T GIVE A FUCK WHAT COLOUR YOUR BUBBLES ARE. If the determining factor in our friendship is whether my text message arrives in the correct fucking colour, we probably weren’t going to make it anyway.

And THAT is what makes Kitty growl. Actually, you know what? I’ve figured it out. It isn’t slow people. It isn’t stupid people. It isn’t confused people. Everybody gets confused. Everybody fucks up. Everybody has a moment. It’s people who somehow make their complete lack of preparation everybody else’s fucking problem.

That’s the growl. I am simply begging humanity to participate in its own fucking life. Know what you want before you get to the speaker. Have your card ready when you get to the window. Read the thing before asking what the thing says. Listen when somebody is talking to you. Don’t start screaming because six customers walked through the door. Don’t bury the important announcement under fourteen fucking TikToks. And if the entire team is standing over THERE, perhaps consider NOT FUCKING OFF OVER HERE.

We have put computers in our pockets. We have robots in our houses. We have people hanging out together in virtual reality from opposite sides of the planet. We have billionaires launching themselves into fucking space. We have access to basically the entire accumulated knowledge of human civilization from a rectangle we carry around in our pocket.

And Brenda is still sitting at the Taco Bell speaker going, “Uhhhhhhhhhh... do you guys have chicken?”

YES, BRENDA. BUT YOU KNOW WHO HAS MORE?

**FUCKING KFC.**
